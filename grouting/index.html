Common questions

What subsurface information is needed before a grouting design can start in Peterborough?

We require borehole logs with recovery and RQD data, packer permeability tests (Lugeon values) in rock, and grain-size distributions from split-spoon samples in overburden. For sites near the Otonabee River, pore pressure monitoring data over at least one seasonal cycle helps define the groundwater regime before selecting injection parameters.

How does fractured limestone in the Peterborough area affect grout take estimates?

The Paleozoic limestone often contains open, solution-widened joints that can accept significantly more grout than a simple rock mass classification would predict. We use packer test results and fracture frequency from oriented core to estimate interconnectivity, then build a grout take model that accounts for radial flow through discrete fractures rather than assuming a homogeneous porous medium.

What does a grouting design package typically cost for a small commercial project in Peterborough?

For a small commercial footprint in Peterborough, a complete grouting design package including site-specific ground model review, injection parameter specification, hole layout, and QA/QC criteria typically falls between CA$1,910 and CA$5,750. The range depends on whether rock grouting, soil grouting, or both are required, and the complexity of the access constraints.

Which grouting method is appropriate for stopping water inflow during a deep excavation?

The choice depends on the formation. In fractured limestone, permeation grouting with microfine cement or ultrafine cement can seal water-bearing joints. In granular soils below the water table, jet grouting or cement-bentonite curtain grouting may be required. We determine the method after reviewing the hydraulic conductivity contrast between the target zone and the surrounding material.
